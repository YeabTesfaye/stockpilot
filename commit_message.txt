Day 6 — "Can we manufacture N units?" (max buildable)

- server/model/max-buildable.ts — deterministic MRP calc: for a product's
  current BOM, compute MIN(floor(available_stock / qty_per_unit)) across every
  material line; returns per-material breakdown + binding constraint flag
- app/api/products/[id]/max-buildable/route.ts — GET endpoint, RLS-scoped via
  withTenant, gated by VIEW_PRODUCTS; returns productId, productName,
  maxBuildable, and per-material rows
- app/(root)/products/[id]/max-buildable/page.tsx — large StatTile for the
  buildable count + DataTable of per-material contribution; binding-constraint
  row flagged with amber accent and a "Binding constraint" StatusBadge
- app/(root)/products/[id]/page.tsx — "Max buildable" button next to "Edit BOM"
  so the count is one click from the product detail view
- seed: raise CHAIR-001 material minStock so the demo computes to exactly 420,
  limited by Backrests (BACK-001); per-material buildable reads
  Seat=2000, Backrest=420, Wheels=1000, Gas Cylinder=4500, Screws=2500,
  Armrest Pair=500
- scripts/smoke-max-buildable.mjs — 13-check smoke test asserting the
  420 figure and the Backrest binding constraint
- docs/decisions.md — Day 6 decision note with expected seed state and
  verification steps
